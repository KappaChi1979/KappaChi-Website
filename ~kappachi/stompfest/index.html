The Stepping Tradition

Stepping is more than just synchronized movement—it's a powerful expression of brotherhood, unity, and cultural heritage. The Kappa Chi Chapter has proudly upheld this tradition, representing Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. with excellence at competitions throughout the Southeast.

Each performance tells our story, embodies our principles of Brotherhood, Scholarship, and Service, and demonstrates the dedication, discipline, and unity that define our chapter. From the countless hours of practice to the electrifying moments on stage, stepping has been a cornerstone of our chapter's identity.

As we continue to build on this legacy, we honor the brothers who came before us and inspire those who will follow. The tradition of excellence continues—The Ball Is Rolling.
